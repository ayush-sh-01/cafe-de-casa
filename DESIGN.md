## Brand & Style

This design system draws its inspiration from the romantic elegance of a sunlit Parisian bistro intertwined with lush botanicals, gilded accents, and artisanal warmth. The identity bridges Old World European hospitality with modern, tactile indulgence. Key architectural touchstones—such as chevron marble floors, delicate gold filigree, ornate gallery art walls, and soft cherry blossom floristry—translate into digital interfaces through restrained luxury, generous negative space, and refined craft.

The aesthetic blends **Tactile / Skeuomorphic subtleties** with **Editorial Minimalism**:
- **Tactility & Heritage:** Gold-bordered card frames reminiscent of antique salon paintings, delicate divider flourishes inspired by wrought ironwork and fleurs-de-lis, and warm parchment-toned surfaces that avoid clinical sterility.
- **Audience & Emotional Response:** Discerning epicures, leisurely diners, and seekers of intimate moments. The experience must feel warm, welcoming, romantic, and distinctly artisanal, evoking the serene pleasure of an afternoon spent reading beside arched windows under soft amber lamplight.

## Colors

The palette captures the warm daylight and golden evening ambiance of the dining room:

- **Primary (`#C5A059` — Antique Gold):** Used for focal accents, luxury borders, key action signifiers, and curated brand highlights. A secondary lustrous tier (`#D4AF37`) provides gilded hover states and micro-interactions.
- **Secondary (`#3E5343` — Botanical Sage / Forest):** Grounded in the surrounding conservatory greenery and leafy veranda views, this deep botanical tone acts as a calming anchor for badges, botanical tags, and secondary interactive moments.
- **Neutral (`#231F1D` — Espresso Bronze):** A rich, roasted dark roast that replaces harsh pure black for all typography, high-contrast borders, and deep nocturnal grounding.
- **Canvas & Surface Foundation:** Layered creams (`#FDFBF7` canvas, `#FAF6EE` surface containers) simulate warm European vellum and honed limestone, mitigating screen glare while establishing an inviting, tactile base.

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy strikes a deliberate balance between literary grandeur and contemporary legibility:

- **Display & Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys the historical elegance of Parisian salon menus and brass-etched signboards. High stroke contrast, graceful serifs, and organic curves create an authentic dining feel. Headings above 28px utilize tighter letter spacing to maintain compositional tension.
- **Body, UI & Numbers (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its clear geometry and warm terminal apertures, ensuring menu descriptions, prices, operational hours, and dietary tags remain effortless to read across all digital touchpoints.
- **Microcopy & Culinary Badges:** Rendered in small-caps with elevated letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`), evoking vintage wine labels and artisanal patisserie stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors classical architectural proportion: structured, rhythmic, and breathing with ease.

- **Grid Architecture:** A 12-column fluid grid system on desktop (`>1024px`) with generous `3rem` section margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Elements reflow to 8 columns on tablet devices (`768px - 1023px`), and condense to a 4-column system with `1.25rem` margins on mobile (`<768px`).
- **Editorial Asymmetry:** Feature modules (such as Chef Specials, Curated Gallery, or Seasonal Cocktails) leverage deliberate alternating columns and gallery offsets inspired by eclectic picture walls.
- **Vertical Cadence:** Generous section gaps (`space-xl` scaled dynamically) provide breathing room reminiscent of bespoke restaurant menus, preventing visual clutter and guiding attention leisurely from dish to narrative.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh, generic drop shadows in favor of **ambient warmth, golden reflections, and layered frames**:

- **Warm Ambient Bloom:** Floating elements (such as reservation drawers, modals, and sticky order strips) utilize multi-stop diffused shadows tinted with warm espresso and amber tones: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(35, 31, 29, 0.10), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08)`.
- **Gilded Borders & Gallery Moldings:** Cards and media containers utilize a hairline 1px antique gold border (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.35)`), creating an intentional picture-frame effect that echoes the bistro’s golden art wall.
- **Frosted Conservatory Glass:** Popovers and navigation bars apply a 12px backdrop blur over a 90% opaque warm ivory base (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.88)`), creating translucency akin to misted conservatory glass panels.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** approach with tailored micro-geometry:

- Standard cards, interactive inputs, and modal dialogs use gentle `0.25rem` to `0.5rem` radii, evoking the refined edges of carved wooden paneling, polished marble slabs, and custom-beveled glass.
- Specialized decorative elements—such as category selector pills and dietary tags—incorporate smooth pill contours to balance the formal geometric grid.
- Hero imagery and selected gallery showcases may integrate subtle Parisian architectural arches (`border-top-left-radius: 9999px; border-top-right-radius: 9999px`) on specific media modules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Rich antique gold fill (`#C5A059`) with espresso text (`#231F1D`), font weight 600, padding `0.75rem 1.75rem`, subtle 1px border in `#D4AF37`. On hover, surface shifts to a radiant golden brass with a gentle amber glow.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Ivory background, 1px espresso bronze border (`#231F1D` or `#C5A059`), matching serif or uppercase sans typography.
- **Botanical / Action:** Deep forest green (`#3E5343`) button with warm cream text for booking tables and nature-inspired ordering actions.

### Cards (Bistro Menu & Gallery)
- Outlined with hairline antique gold borders (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.4)`), set upon parchment ivory (`#FAF6EE`).
- Menu item cards feature a subtle dot-leader separation connecting the item title to the artisanal price token, paired with soft botanical descriptions below.
- Photo cards emulate framed paintings with an inner 4px inset margin and a 1px brass perimeter.

### Chips & Dietary Badges
- Compact capsules (`rounded-full`) with a soft sage background (`rgba(62, 83, 67, 0.1)`) and forest green text (`#2C3E30`) for vegan, organic, or specialty origin highlights.
- Gilded chef recommendation badges use an ivory ground with a 1px gold border and `#C5A059` lettering.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background in spotless warm vellum (`#FDFBF7`), bottom border or full 1px border in muted brass (`#C5A059` at 40% opacity).
- Focused state transitions smoothly to full antique gold with an inner warm glow; labels transition into small-caps botanical styling.

### Flourishes & Dividers
- Section dividers incorporate subtle centered filigree flourishes or fleur-de-lis motifs in antique gold, breaking up long tasting menus and narrative passages with European charm.
